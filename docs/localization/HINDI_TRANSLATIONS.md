# Sorting Puzzle — Hindi Translations

Locale: `hi` · Prepared October 6, 2026

Hindi translations of all **251 inventory entries**, with the original English and keys for review. The six plural-sensitive entries have `one` and `other` forms in the appendix; the main tables show the `other` form.

The copy uses friendly, concise Hindi, with familiar mobile-game terms such as लेवल, कॉम्बो, बूस्टर and लाइफ़. Devanagari has no uppercase/lowercase distinction, so titles use the same letter forms as ordinary text. Preserve their existing title styling when integrating these translations.

Placeholders (`%d`, `%s`), their order, symbols and the source inventory's escaped line-break notation are preserved. `AD`, `OFF` and the app name `SortingPuzzle` remain unchanged. Percentage discount tags retain their existing digits, `%` and `OFF`. Player names, native language names, platform names, email addresses, reference codes and store-formatted prices remain unchanged.

## Hindi plural forms

Hindi has two cardinal categories, `one` and `other`. For the game's nonnegative integer counts, `one` covers **0 and 1**, and `other` covers **2 and above**, following [Unicode CLDR's Hindi plural rules](https://www.unicode.org/cldr/charts/48/supplemental/language_plural_rules.html). Select the category from the raw numeric count, before formatting it as `%s`. These are cardinal categories, not a rule that every Hindi noun must change spelling.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Review notes

- The compact home badge is `पैक`; the full starter-offer title is `शुरुआती पैक`.
- The chapter names are `किराना नगर` and `कैंडी का कोना`.
- `common.continue` is `आगे बढ़ें` for moving on, while `common.resume` is `खेल जारी रखें` for returning to the active level. Retrying a load is `फिर लोड करें`; replaying a failed level is `फिर खेलें`.
- `लाइफ़` is the familiar gaming term for the heart-based life counter. It retains its spelling with different counts.
- Short time units are `से.` (seconds), `मि.` (minutes), `घं.` (hours) and `दि.` (days). The `×`, `+`, `#`, `·` and `•` symbols and numbered ad-report steps are preserved.
- Numbers explicitly written in the source, including 15 seconds, 30 seconds and the 30-day deletion period, are retained. A date inserted into `deletion.accepted.body_deadline` should be formatted in Hindi; an example is `2 नवंबर 2026`. The `%s` placeholder stays unchanged.
- Future runtime integration needs a font that covers Devanagari and renders its joined letters and vowel marks correctly. Long modal titles, `लीडरबोर्ड`, `साप्ताहिक`, support action buttons and two-line ad buttons need rendered layout review at the project's supported resolutions. This document has not been visually tested in the game.
