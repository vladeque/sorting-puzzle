| Information | Examples and sources |

## 4. Optional advertisements and your choices

Where available, the game offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Watching an ad is
your choice. Our launch configuration requests non-personalized/contextual ads rather than ads
selected using an interest profile.

Non-personalized ads still involve personal data. Google's advertising SDK may process your IP
address and approximate location, app/device information, identifiers such as Android advertising
ID or app set ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement,
and fraud prevention. Identifier use depends on the platform, permissions, and configuration.
Our iOS launch configuration disables IDFA access and cross-app tracking. Consent tools also
process your privacy choices.

For reward verification, we send an opaque reference identifying the reward request. We do not
send your display name, email address, raw game-account identifier, or gameplay contents in that
reference. Our backend does not run a separate behavioral-analytics or attribution service;
operational diagnostics and Google's advertising measurement still occur as described above.

When required, we ask for your advertising privacy choices before enabling the relevant
processing. You can revisit them through **Settings → Privacy Choices** when that option is
available. You can also use your device's advertising-identifier controls. Declining consent
does not prevent ordinary gameplay; an ad-dependent reward may be unavailable.

Read [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for its processing. This
policy explains our own responsibilities; Google's notice does not replace them.

## 8. Delete your account or local game data

For an online account, including a registered guest account, open **Settings → Support → Delete
Account** and follow the confirmation steps. You can also request deletion outside the game at
**[ACCOUNT_DELETION_REQUEST_URL]**, or contact **sortingpuzzle.support@gmail.com** if you cannot
access it.

Once your deletion request is accepted, access to the account is revoked and it is removed from
online leaderboard results. We complete removal or de-identification of the account's login,
profile, and associated game data within **30 days**, subject to the limited retained records and
backup period in section 7.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be cancelled after acceptance. Deleted progress and rewards
cannot be recovered by signing into the same platform account. You can start a new, separate game
account. Deleting your game account does not delete your Google or Apple account or their
independent purchase records; refund requests follow the applicable store process.

If your installation has only local game data and has never registered an online account, the
action is labeled **Delete local game data**. It clears that installation's game data. Uninstalling
the app, signing out, or clearing local files does not by itself delete an existing server account.
